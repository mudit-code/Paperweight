WHAT IT DOES

Five jobs, one app.

01
Scan to PDF
Scan with the camera, or share photos from your gallery straight into Paperweight. Edit, then save as a PDF. All offline.
02
Compress
Pick a level, compress many PDFs and images at once, and see the result right there. Save as a ZIP or as separate files, and share the same way.
03
Resize by cropping
Set the width and height you need, then frame your photo with a cropper. You get exactly that size, with no stretching and no guessing at settings.
04
Merge
Combine PDFs and images into one document. Drag to reorder, name the file, done.
05
Convert
PDF to images, images to PDF, or any image format to another.
PRIVACY

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing uploaded.

Paperweight works with zero network requests. On Android it asks for no storage permission: it only sees the files you share with it, and they stay inside the app on your device.

On-device execution · Camera only for scanning · Works identically in airplane mode.
